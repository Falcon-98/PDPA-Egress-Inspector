Privacy policy

PDPA Egress Inspector, a Chrome extension. Last updated 31 August 2026.

The short version

This extension sends nothing anywhere. It has no server, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, telemetry or advertising code. Everything it reads stays on your computer, and the values it records are masked before they are stored.

What the extension reads

On sites you have explicitly added, and only on those sites, it examines:

  • text you paste into a field
  • text you type into a field, a short moment after you stop typing
  • text you copy or cut from the page
  • the contents of plain-text files you attach (.txt, .csv, .json, .log, .sql and similar), and the filename and size only of other formats
  • the values of a form at the moment you submit it

Password fields are skipped entirely and are never read. No site is examined until you add it in the extension's settings, at which point Chrome asks you to grant access to that one site. Removing the site withdraws that access immediately.

What is stored, and where

All storage is Chrome's own extension storage on your device.

  • Settings — the sites you added, your policy choice, your internal hostnames. Stored in chrome.storage.sync, which means Chrome may sync them between your own signed-in browsers, under Google's terms rather than ours.
  • Findings log — up to 500 recent events, stored in chrome.storage.local, which never syncs and never leaves the device. Each event records the time, the hostname, the page path (never the query string), the channel, the destination category, a risk score, and which categories were detected.

Detected values are masked at the point of detection, inside the tab, before anything is handed to the rest of the extension. A National Identity Card number recorded as 923451234V is stored as 92••••••4V. The full value is never written to storage and never held after the check completes.

What is never collected

  • Nothing is transmitted to the developer or to any third party, because nothing is transmitted at all.
  • No browsing history, no page contents, and no data from sites you have not added.
  • No account, sign-in, email address or device identifier. The extension does not know who you are.
  • No data is sold, transferred or used for advertising, credit assessment or lending.

Your control

  • Remove a site in settings and inspection of it stops at once, along with Chrome's permission for it.
  • Pause the inspector entirely from settings without removing any site.
  • Clear the findings log at any time from the popup or from settings.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes its local storage, including the whole log.

If you deploy this to a team

Running this extension on staff browsers is itself the processing of personal data, and the organisation that deploys it is the controller of that processing. Tell staff in writing before deployment, record your lawful basis, and decide in advance who may read the log. The log is masked precisely so that reviewing it does not require access to the underlying personal data.

Children

This is a workplace compliance tool and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what is read or stored, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
